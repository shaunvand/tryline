# Official competition and union apps (scout, 2026-11-07)

Method: App Store figures come from the iTunes lookup/search API and the iTunes customer-review RSS feed (GB storefront unless stated). Google Play pages did not render for fetching, so Android numbers come from third-party trackers and are flagged. Complaint lists are my tally of the most recent ~12 reviews in each feed (small sample, not the full review base). Every app below is free to download; no subscription tier was found inside the first-party competition apps except where stated.

## Headline findings
- No official Top 14 / LNR consumer app and no dedicated Super Rugby Pacific app were found. Fans are pushed to broadcasters, RugbyPass, or third-party apps. This is a gap for TryLine. ([LNR search result](https://www.prnewswire.com/fr/news-releases/la-ligue-nationale-du-rugby-lnr-marque-des-points-grace-a-lautomatisation-des-processus-dappian-301864030.html), [NZR app is the closest for Super Rugby](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nzr/id6450051979)). Absence is based on searches, not proof.
- Best-rated first-party apps: Six Nations (4.68, 13,800 ratings) and PREM Rugby (4.56, 3,396). URC is weak (iOS 3.16 on 19 ratings; Android about 2.9 stars).
- Recurring pain across all: login walls, wrong or stale data, slow score updates, cluttered layouts.

## URC (United Rugby Championship)
- iOS "United Rugby Championship", seller Celtic Rugby DAC, free, version 2026.9.6 released 2026-09-26, rating 3.16 from 19 ratings (GB store). Source: [iTunes lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1436694463&country=gb).
- Features per listing: club and league news, match previews and reports, match centre with line-ups, tables, fixtures, live text commentary with statistics, favourite-club alerts, fantasy and predictor games, polls, on-demand highlights. Source: [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1436694463).
- Android: package org.pro14rugby.app, about 121K installs, 2.88 stars from 514 ratings, last updated 2026-09-26 (third-party tracker, not Play itself). Source: [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/org.pro14rugby.app). A search snippet of the same tracker showed 1.76 from 478 ratings (older snapshot).
- Streaming is a separate app, URC TV (tv.urc): about 48.3K installs, 1.56 stars from 149 ratings, last update 2025-09-25 (tracker). Source: [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/tv.urc).
- Top 3 complaints (iOS GB, latest 12): (1) crashes, slowness, dropouts after updates; (2) login failures, Apple sign-in broken, reset emails not arriving; (3) lost features (notifications, European Champions/Challenge Cup fixtures, fantasy line-up selection). Source: [review feed](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/rss/customerreviews/id=1436694463/sortby=mostrecent/json).

## Premiership Rugby (PREM Rugby app)
- iOS "PREM Rugby", seller Premier Rugby Limited, free, version 3.18.2 released 2026-11-03, rating 4.56 from 3,396 ratings. Source: [iTunes lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1148123668&country=gb).
- Features: club and league news, fixtures and match centre with live scores and tables, exclusive video, personalised club notifications, Gallagher PREM Final coverage and tickets. Source: [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1148123668).
- Monetisation: app is free; one reviewer says they pay for TNT but cannot get live games in-app (no price found). Android listing not verified.
- Top 3 complaints (latest 12): (1) cannot find, buy or watch live matches; (2) live scores hard to read (black on red) and cluttered layout; (3) ticket redemption flaky and scores not updating at half time. One user also asks for a fantasy game. Source: [review feed](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/rss/customerreviews/id=1148123668/sortby=mostrecent/json).

## Six Nations
- iOS "Six Nations Rugby Official", Six Nations Rugby Ltd, free, version 8.15.0 released 2026-08-27, rating 4.68 from 13,800 ratings. Source: [iTunes lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=489164343&country=gb).
- Features: live match centre, Fantasy Rugby, highlights and exclusive video, team news, fixtures and stats, table, player profiles, quizzes, ticket competitions, live match notifications, Autumn Nations Series; English, French, Italian. Source: [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/six-nations-official/id489164343).
- Top 3 complaints (latest 12): (1) missing or hard-to-find info (line-ups not loading, no TV channel info); (2) incorrect data (squads, positions, stats, wrong photo, wrong points scenario); (3) fantasy notifications that continue after being switched off, plus logouts. Source: [review feed](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/rss/customerreviews/id=489164343/sortby=mostrecent/json).
- Android: the Six Nations site links a Google Play version ([site](https://sixnationsrugby.com/en/m6n/news/six-nations-rugby-app-bringing-you-closer-to-the-game)); rating not verified.

## World Rugby / Rugby World Cup
- iOS "Men's Rugby World Cup 2027", World Rugby Limited, free, last release 2025-12-01, rating 4.71 from 9,101 ratings (GB store). Source: [iTunes search](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=world%20rugby&country=gb&entity=software&limit=25).
- Other World Rugby apps: HSBC SVNS 4.43 from 3,229 ratings, updated 2025-11-24; Laws of Rugby 4.21 from 14 ratings (same source). RWC feature list not verified.
- Top 3 complaints (latest 12): (1) login and registration problems (no autofill or Face ID, fantasy keeps logging out); (2) slow or wrong data (reversed score, lagging live scores); (3) missing features and forced web redirects (player info only in browser, no past tournaments). Source: [review feed](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/rss/customerreviews/id=1436008780/sortby=mostrecent/json).

## SA Rugby / Springboks
- iOS "Springboks", seller Ticketmaster UK Ltd, free, version 1.35.0, first released 2026-06-09, updated 2026-11-04, 4.0 stars from 89 ratings (ZA store). Source: [iTunes lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=6760295918&country=za).
- It is a ticketing app (discover games, buy, resell, transfer, entry), not a scores or stats app. Same source. A 2026 report says fans struggled to buy tickets through the Ticketmaster link ([Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-08-20-springboks-ticket-strategy-leaves-fans-frustrated/)).

## Super Rugby Pacific and The Rugby Championship
- No dedicated Super Rugby Pacific app found. NZR's "All Blacks & Black Ferns Rugby" (NZR+): free, version 11.0.9 released 2026-09-21, about 3.98 stars from 310 ratings (NZ store). Features: live match centre, team and player profiles, highlights, behind-the-scenes content, archive, news, fixtures. Source: [iTunes lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=6450051979&country=nz). Listing mentions a Gaming tab with free Super Rugby Pacific predictors ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nzr/id6450051979)).
- Viewing is via broadcasters: Stan Sport in Australia ([Reds](https://reds.rugby/experience/watch)), Sky Sport/ThreeNow in NZ ([Planet Rugby](https://www.planetrugby.com/news/super-rugby-pacific-how-to-watch-the-action-as-2026-season-set-to-kick-off)).
- Rugby Championship: no dedicated app found.
- Aggregator: Union Live (Sportsmate), free, 4.44 stars from 11,466 ratings (AU store), updated 2026-07-16 ([iTunes search](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=super%20rugby%20pacific&country=au&entity=software&limit=25)). Its GB reviews complain of stale content, missing internationals and Rugby Championship, and ad/podcast clutter; newest review dated 2024-11-11 ([reviews](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/rss/customerreviews/id=416969111/sortby=mostrecent/json)).

## Adjacent: RugbyPass
- "RugbyPass - Live Scores & News": free, 4.50 from 1,686 ratings (GB), updated 2026-10-28 ([iTunes search](https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=world%20rugby&country=gb&entity=software&limit=25)). Subscription price not verified.
- Top 3 complaints (latest 10): login loops via World Rugby account, streams cutting out or freezing, hard to find live events ([review feed](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/rss/customerreviews/id=6456941954/sortby=mostrecent/json)).

## Implications for TryLine
- Evidence-backed differentiators: no login, fast live scores, readable score colours, correct line-ups, notification control.
- Top 14 and Super Rugby in one place is unserved by official apps.
- Not verified: Google Play ratings for PREM, Six Nations, RWC; Springboks app on Play; subscription prices for RugbyPass and NZR+.
