# Live-score and broadcaster apps with rugby coverage (scout, 2026-11-07)

Coverage is partial. Time-boxed. Items marked UNVERIFIED were not confirmed on a primary page. Not covered at all: theScore, OneFootball, LiveScore rugby depth, SuperSport app store ratings, ESPN Scrum heritage (only one old RugbyNow link seen).

## Dedicated rugby competitors (most relevant to TryLine)
- **Gainline: Rugby Live Scores** (iOS, developer Red Davis): 4.6 stars from 18 ratings on the US App Store. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6642677437)
  - Features: Six Nations, RWC, Premiership, URC, Top 14, Super Rugby; minute-by-minute timelines (tries, conversions, penalties, cards); lineups, post-match summaries, match stats; Match Insights, Referee Watch, player match ratings, weather, head-to-head, broadcast info by country; Lock Screen Live Activities and widgets; customizable kick-off/score/full-time alerts. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6642677437)
  - Monetisation: free; Gainline Pro subscriptions at USD 2.99 and 6.99 per month, USD 19.99 and 39.99 per year. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6642677437)
  - Complaint: lists defunct MLR teams and lacks some fixtures such as the Nations Cup. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6642677437)
  - Note: this is the closest feature overlap with TryLine and already ships ratings, lineups and Live Activities. It has no web/PWA and is iOS-only as far as the listing shows (estimate).
- **RugbyPass TV**: 4.6 stars from 226 ratings (South Africa App Store). [App Store ZA](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/rugbypass-tv/id6456941954)
  - Features: live streaming (SVNS, U20, Pacific Nations Cup), rugby news, database with live scores, stats, tables, lineups, player profiles; Match Centre team sheets; push notifications for followed teams/players; Chromecast; option to hide live scores. [App Store ZA](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/rugbypass-tv/id6456941954)
  - Price: app free, no IAP prices on the page. An undated help article gave USD 7.99/week, 19.99/month, 159.99/year (may be stale). [search summary, unverified]
  - Complaints: 2023 reviewer said store page did not make clear only selected RWC matches were available in selected locations; 2024 login loop, and the developer said VPN users cannot sign in; wish for more club coverage. [App Store ZA](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/rugbypass-tv/id6456941954)
  - A third-party tracker lists 4.8 from 646 reviews, updated 2026-02-09 (different storefront/date). [AppPricingLab](https://apppricinglab.com/app/apple/6456941954)

## Implications for TryLine (estimate)
- Rugby-specific apps (Gainline, RugbyPass) have lineups, ratings and notifications; a free, no-install PWA with pitch zone, win probability and replay is the differentiator.
- Generalist apps handle rugby lineups poorly (Flashscore ordering complaint); correct position ordering is a cheap win.
- Broadcaster pricing is high (R399 to R799, GBP 31): a free second screen sits alongside, not against, them.
