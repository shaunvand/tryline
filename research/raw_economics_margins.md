# TryLine: economics of sports companion apps (raw research, 2026-11-07)

Scope note: WebSearch snippets plus a few WebFetch reads. Items marked "(snippet only)" were not re-read on the cited page. "(estimate)" marks inferred numbers. Several competitor figures are third-party estimates, flagged as such.

## Public numbers for the big score apps
- FotMob (Norway), fiscal year 2024 per iGamingToday, which cites public filings: revenue NOK 129,052,000 (about EUR 11.0M), EBITDA NOK 56,727,000, pre-tax profit NOK 63,579,000, 29 employees, over 20M MAU; ads are the main revenue, FotMob Premium in-app purchase is second. Source: [iGamingToday](https://www.igamingtoday.com/fotmob-the-norwegian-success-story-youve-probably-never-heard-of/). Implied pre-tax margin about 49% (estimate); EBITDA margin about 44% (estimate). Pre-tax profit above EBITDA is odd and unexplained by the article; treat cautiously.
- FotMob's own 2026 ad deck claims 21M monthly users ([FotMob advertising overview 2026](https://data.fotmob.com/documents/media-kit/FotMob%20Advertising%20Overview%202026%20v2.pdf), snippet only). Revenue per MAU about USD 0.55-0.60 per year (estimate, from NOK 129M / 20M MAU).
- Third-party revenue guesses for FotMob run from USD 5.6M ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/c/fotmob)) to USD 8.6M ([Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/FotMob)); both are below the filing-based figure, so they are unreliable.
- SofaScore (Croatia): no audited 2024 figures found. Similarweb lists USD 10-15M annual revenue ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/company/sofascore.com), snippet only, third-party). Company says 40M MAU ([Sofascore news](https://www.sofascore.com/news/sofascore-among-top-sports-apps-in-2026)); 6th most-downloaded sports app of 2024 ([Sofascore](https://www.sofascore.com/news/?p=71674)); Sensor Tower State of Mobile 2026 puts it 3rd by MAU and 6th by in-app revenue (company-reported, same Sofascore page). Older profile: revenue doubled to HRK 83M ([Lider](https://en.lider.media/?p=33004), undated snippet). It also runs a large betting affiliate arm ([SBC News](https://sbcnews.co.uk/europe/2024/07/05/sofascore-affiliate-leaders/), snippet only).
- Not found: audited LiveScore and Flashscore revenue. Next step: Croatian and Norwegian registry filings.

## Illustrative unit economics (all inputs are assumptions)
Assumptions (estimate): 8 sessions per MAU per month; 3 ad impressions per session (24 per MAU per month); blended eCPM USD 0.80 (between SA and UK ranges above); premium USD 3.99/month, 0.5% of MAU convert, 15% store fee, net USD 3.39; affiliate: 0.1% of MAU make a first deposit per month at USD 60 CPA (inside the USD 25-90 range above); hosting USD 20 / 100 / 800 per month; free data feeds. Seasonality and betting-ad legality ignored.

- 10k MAU, monthly: ads USD 192; premium 50 subs, USD 170; affiliate 10 first deposits, USD 600 (all estimate). Annual: USD 2.3k / 2.0k / 7.2k (estimate).
- 100k MAU, monthly: ads USD 1,920; premium 500 subs, USD 1,695; affiliate 100 first deposits, USD 6,000 (all estimate). Annual: USD 23k / 20k / 72k (estimate).
- 1M MAU, monthly: ads USD 19,200; premium 5,000 subs, USD 16,950; affiliate 1,000 first deposits, USD 60,000 (all estimate). Annual: USD 230k / 203k / 720k (estimate).
- Sanity check: ad yield of about USD 0.23 per MAU per year (estimate) is below FotMob's implied USD 0.55-0.60 (estimate); plausible for a thin-engagement, SA-skewed audience.
- Margin: hosting-only costs give about 90% ads margin at 100k+ MAU (estimate); at 10k, USD 20/month hosting is about 10% of ads revenue (estimate). The real cost is founder time.
- Takeaway (estimate): affiliate yields about 3x ads or premium per MAU if legally allowed; ads plus an ad-free upsell is the safe baseline. USD 1k/month needs about 50k MAU on ads alone (estimate).
