# Raw complaints and praise: rugby and live-score apps (2026-11-07)

Method note: Reddit (r/rugbyunion etc.), Google Play pages, Keo.co.za, Planet Rugby, The Silver Fern and Rugby365 comments were NOT reachable. WebFetch refuses reddit.com and searches returned no forum threads. All evidence below is from App Store pages (only about 4 reviews are visible per page), one Trustpilot page, and search summaries. Sample sizes are tiny, so frequencies are directional only. Gap: no Reddit or forum evidence; recommend a manual Reddit pass.

## Themes ranked by frequency (across reviews seen)

### 1. Missing or disappearing competitions and fixtures (5 reviews, 3 apps)
- Union Live, 2024-07-14: "You keep changing the coverage you have, sometimes there's international matches, then they're gone!!" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/union-live/id416969111))
- Union Live, 2023-09-30: "it used to have every union competition under the sun on it"; the reviewer said the 2023 World Cup was missing so they downloaded another app ([source](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/union-live/id416969111)).
- Union Live, 2020-11-13: Rugby Championship fixtures not added; NPC/Mitre 10 Cup not covered ([source](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/union-live/id416969111)).
- Gainline, July (year not shown): "it doesn't have all the fixtures such as the Nations Cup"; also lists folded MLR teams ([source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6642677437)).
- Ultimate Rugby, search summary only: Premiership and Top 14 live coverage reduced to replacements and scores, no card detail ([secondary, unverified](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/ultimate-rugby/id565995979)).

### 2. Paywalls and subscription friction (3 verified reviews, Ultimate Rugby)
- 2017-10-14: "Subscription nonsense"; subscription needed to see lower-ranked series, "ridiculous" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id565995979)).
- 2018-11-18: "Data that was free is now moved behind the paywall"; "Shortsighted and greedy" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id565995979)).
- 2020-02-08: "I have to keep clicking restore content"; "verging on a scam type app" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id565995979)).
- Sofascore: aggregators report a "growing stat paywall" ([secondary, not verified](https://marlvel.ai/apps/sofascore-live-sports-scores/reviews)).

### 3. Intrusive ads and pop-ups (5 reviews, 3 apps)
- Ultimate Rugby, 2024-11-08: "unwanted videos constantly pop up" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id565995979)).
- Ultimate Rugby, 2017-10-14: "way too many advertisements" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id565995979)).
- Sofascore, 2024-04-03: "the ad placement is very intrusive" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1176147574)).
- Flashscore (Trustpilot), 2026-07-07, 1 star: "So many ads inappropriately force you to other sites" ([source](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/flashscore.com)).
- Sofascore, 2025-03-11: odds tab not hidden even with "Hide Odds" set ([source](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1176147574)).

### 4. Wrong, reversing or slow score data (Flashscore, football-heavy; pattern applies to rugby)
- 2026-09-15, 1 star: "suddenly 1-0 but there is no goal and after 10 min back to 0-0" ([source](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/flashscore.com)).
- 2026-10-08, 1 star: "Constantly shows a goal only to correct after a few minutes!" ([source](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/flashscore.com))
- 2026-04-25, 2 star: "This app is very slow when there is games on, always loading and can't see the scores." ([source](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/flashscore.com))
- Ultimate Rugby, 2024-11-08: news and data "seems to always be inaccurate in a few details" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id565995979)).

### 5. Streaming and connection failures during busy events (3 verified reviews)
- RugbyPass, 2025-03-30: "Nice when it works, which is rare... seems to shut down every time there is an hsbc tournament" (star rating not shown) ([source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rugbypass-live-scores-news/id6456941954)).
- RugbyPass, 2025-02-22: "connection failed" and "no content available" across iPad, phone, TV ([source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rugbypass-live-scores-news/id6456941954)).
- SuperSport, 2017-11-25: recurring connection error with or without wifi; reviewer moved to Twitter ([source](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/supersport/id356818029)).

### 6. Redesign regressions and weak UX (4 reviews)
- SuperSport, 2021-03-24: simple tasks take too many steps ([source](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/supersport/id356818029)).
- SuperSport, 2020-10-10: names cut off, text too large, wants the old version back ([source](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/supersport/id356818029)).
- Sofascore, 2025-03-11: "Slow decline of an otherwise great app"; redesign hurt match-detail readability ([source](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1176147574)).
- 6 Nations app, 2019-02-02: unclear which games are live, "Very vague" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id414001342)).

### 7. Notifications and reminders (thin evidence)
- SuperSport: setting a reminder cancels the one already set; no indicator that a reminder exists ([source](https://apps.apple.com/za/app/supersport/id356818029)).
- Flashscore, 2026-07-07: "every 5 seconds you get pop up messages saying something went wrong" ([source](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/flashscore.com)).
- No review found about late rugby notifications or battery. Not evidenced; do not claim these as fan complaints.

### 8. Pro-only narrow scope
- Union Live, 2020-02-21: "it only covers the 0.5 per cent of players who are professional" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/union-live/id416969111)). Not relevant to TryLine scope.

## What fans PRAISE (keep)
- One place for all rugby: Ultimate Rugby "the only thing out there" that puts all rugby info in one place (2024-11-08) ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id565995979)).
- Speed and rugby-specific design: Gainline praised for speed, intuitive design and rugby focus; "great to see an app solely dedicated to following and recording stats for rugby" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6642677437)).
- Simplicity beats official apps: 6 Nations app "Much more user friendly than the official Guinness app" (2024-04-02); "Basic but what I need to keep track of game times and game stats" ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id414001342)).
- Free Lock Screen Live Activities requested by a RugbyPass reviewer (July) ([source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/rugbypass-live-scores-news/id6456941954)).
- Independent stats and configurable alerts praised on Sofascore ([source](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1176147574)).
- Requested feature: iCal fixture subscription (6 Nations app review) ([source](https://apps.apple.com/app/id414001342)).

## Implications for TryLine (estimate)
- Stable competition coverage, honest labeling of what is covered, and no paywall or pop-up ads match the top three complaint themes (estimate: about 15 of about 27 low-star items seen).
- Show a clear "live now" state and data freshness stamp; avoid score flicker with reversals (themes 4 and 6).
