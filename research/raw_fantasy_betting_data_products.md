## Fantasy rugby

- **Official Guinness Six Nations Fantasy** (fantasy.sixnationsrugby.com/m6n). 200-star cap, 15 starters + 1 Supersub, max 4 per country, Captain doubles points, Supersub trebles; unlimited free transfers. Scoring includes tries, try assists, defenders beaten, carried metres (1 per 10m), 50-22 (7), tackles, breakdown steals, lineout steals, penalty/card deductions, Player of the Match 15. Prices move during the tournament. [source](https://fantasyrugbygeek.com/fantasy-rugby-games-overview-official-guinness-six-nations-2026-fantasy-game/). Price of entry not stated there. Upgraded UI shows each player's ownership percentage (same source).
- **Six Nations Official app (iOS)**: free, 4.7 from 932 ratings on the US App Store; lists Live match centre, Fantasy Rugby, highlights, fixtures/stats, table, player profiles, quizzes, live-match notifications; English/French/Italian. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id489164343). Android: 3.28 stars from about 1.3K ratings per a third-party tracker (AppGoblin, not Google Play directly) [source](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.sixnationsrugby). Implication: the Android experience is weak, an opening for a simpler live experience.
- **Superbru**: free web + iOS/Android predictor and fantasy games across Six Nations, URC, Champions Cup, Super Rugby Pacific, Premiership, RWC. The 2026 Six Nations fantasy edition is shown as not currently active. [Superbru Six Nations Fantasy](https://www.superbru.com/sixnationsfantasy/), [Superbru rugby hub](https://www.superbru.com/home/sport.php?back=1&s=1). A 4.8 App Store claim appears only on Superbru's own page; unverified.
- **DraftXV**: free head-to-head draft fantasy for URC (up to 12 managers) and Premiership (up to 10): live draft room with pick timer, trades, waivers, playoffs, and a running live matchup score during games. [DraftXV](https://www.draftxv.com/)
- **Other games** listed by a fantasy guide: official Super Rugby Pacific fantasy, Dream XV Rugby (Premiership), Draft Rugby (Super Rugby Pacific and URC); not opened. [Fantasy Rugby Geek resources](https://fantasyrugbygeek.com/resources/). Gainline is an iPhone fantasy-companion app with a premium tier, mentioned as a prize in [Fantasy Rugby Geek](https://fantasyrugbygeek.com/fantasy-rugby-games-overview-official-guinness-six-nations-2026-fantasy-game/); not otherwise checked.
- **Audience**: casual fans and friend leagues; the key live need is "how many fantasy points is my player on right now".
- **Borrowable**: per-player live stat lines (carries, metres, tackles, steals, 50-22, cards) in a points-style view; top-performer ranking; match notifications; a "my players" watchlist with no account (local storage).

## Consumer stats and player ratings

- **RugbyPass Index (RPI)**: machine-learning ratings of players and teams based on winning and individual contribution to winning, built with Dot Loves Data; launched 2 Nov 2018 covering nearly 3,000 pros; hosted at index.rugbypass.com. Free/paid status not stated. [RugbyPass](https://rugbypass.com/news/rugbypass-index-rpi-officially-launched). Current status not verified.
- **Opta Index / The Analyst**: video-analysed touch-by-touch data; each action gets a points value; weekly "European rugby team of the week" articles for Premiership, Top 14, URC; Jalibert top scorer in 2025-26. [The Analyst](https://theanalyst.com/articles/european-rugby-team-of-the-week-jalibert-takes-top-spot). Editorial content; price not found.
- **Borrowable**: a post-match top-performer list computed from free feed stats, labelled as own formula, plus a team-of-the-week style recap.

## Data/analytics providers and coaching tools

- **Oval Insights**: data provider to Premiership, URC, European Cup, Sky Sports, Channel 9; live APIs such as in-game win predictor and kick success likelihood, delivered via AWS to broadcasters. B2B, no consumer price. [Broadcast](https://www.broadcastnow.co.uk/production/aws-and-oval-insights-unveil-data-driven-rugby-insights/5186253.article), [TVBEurope](https://www.tvbeurope.com/?p=114679). Implication: win probability is a broadcast-grade feature, so label TryLine's as a simple model.
- **Smart Rugby**: no Oval-related product found. The only hit was Sportable's smart ball trial in Premiership Rugby [SportBusiness](https://sportbusiness.com/news/premiership-rugby-to-trial-sportables-smart-ball-tech-in-northern-hemisphere-first).
- **Hudl Sportscode**: price by quote; third-party estimated annual licences about £1,000 (Gamebreaker), £1,500, £2,500, £3,000 (Pro), £5,000 (Elite) (estimate, from a Nacsport reseller so biased). Top-level teams. [AnalysisPro](https://www.analysispro.com/nacsport-vs-hudl-sportscode)
- **Nacsport**: annual licences ex VAT £125 Basic, £240 Basic+, £595 Scout, £995 Pro, £1,845 Elite; lifetime from £520. Amateurs to pros. [AnalysisPro](https://www.analysispro.com/nacsport-vs-hudl-sportscode)
- **Catapult**: Catapult One from $179.99/year per a listing [Fitgap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/055754/stats-hosted-solutions); a competitor page claims $70,000/year with hardware (unsourced marketing, low confidence). Full Vector price not published.
- **Free analysis tools**: Kinovea (slow-mo, frame-by-frame) and LongoMatch (event tagging, clip export) have free tiers. [Frame Sports](https://framesports.ai/blog/free-vs-paid-rugby-analysis-software)
- **Audience**: coaches and analysts, not fans; low overlap. Borrow only event-tagged timeline and exportable match summary ideas.

## Decision-relevant takeaways
- Official Six Nations app already has a live match centre plus fantasy, but Android rating is weak; a per-player live points view is the main borrowable idea.
- No betting tracker found with pitch-zone or replay; try alerts are table stakes.
- Win probability is a broadcast B2B feature (Oval); free in TryLine is a differentiator, label it as a model estimate.
